The numbers you need, right where you need them. Simple controls keep you close to the task at hand.

01 / RECORD

One tap. Back to practice.

Tap Record rep in simple mode, or Success and Failure in outcome mode. Keep count from your wrist, then get ready for the next one.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Know your numbers.

See selected-value and whole-session counts; outcome mode also shows your success rate.

03 / ADJUST

Move the challenge.

Adjust a distance, weight or duration setting with touch or swipe; change compatible units without relabelling recorded events. Unitless counting has no artificial quantity.

04 / CORRECT

Mistapped? Undo it.

Undo removes your last recorded attempt. Correct a slip, then carry on with your practice.

Set. Tap. Repeat.

A simple rhythm for your practice.

  1. 01

    Set up practice

    Choose simple or outcome mode and a single measurement type, then start a session.

  2. 02

    Record events

    Tap the appropriate recording control after each event. Undo the last entry if needed.

  3. 03

    Save the session

    Finish to save grouped totals, then reopen history to inspect or delete a summary.
